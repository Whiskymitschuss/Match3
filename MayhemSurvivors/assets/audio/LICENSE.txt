RF Worlds — Mayhem Survivors audio
All effects and both musical compositions are original procedural creations for this project. No external samples or recordings. Generator: tools/generate_presentation_audio.py (numpy/scipy). Earlier generators remain for provenance; running them overwrites older cue versions. Use the presentation generator to regenerate 0.8 audio.
